Civic Authority — Brand Guidelines
Project: Integrated Sangguniang Kabataan Digital Governance, Planning, Transparency, and Youth Engagement Platform
Location: Olongapo City, Philippines
Brand direction: Civic Authority
Tagline: Empowering Youth. Building Communities.
1. Brand Identity
The Civic Authority visual identity balances public-sector credibility with youth-focused energy. It should feel trustworthy, transparent, modern, accessible, and community-centered.
Core principles
- Trust: Clear information hierarchy and a stable navy foundation.
- Transparency: Open layouts, readable reports, and straightforward navigation.
- Youth participation: Emerald accents and approachable language.
- Community impact: Real project imagery and clear evidence of progress.
- Accessibility: Strong contrast, readable typography, and responsive layouts.
2. Color Palette
Token	Color name	Hex	Recommended use
Primary	Deep Governance Navy	#142B4A	Brand foundation, headings, navbar, footer, dark hero sections
Secondary	Emerald Green	#16845B	Primary buttons, active states, links, key actions
Accent	Civic Gold	#E5B64B	Small highlights, milestones, decorative details
Background	Soft Gray	#F5F7FA	Page backgrounds and alternating sections
Surface	Pure White	#FFFFFF	Cards, forms, content panels
Text muted	Slate Gray	#64748B	Supporting text and descriptions
Border	Border Gray	#E2E8F0	Card borders, dividers, input outlines


Color usage guidance
- Use navy for the strongest visual anchors: headings, navigation, and footer.
- Use emerald for primary calls to action and selected/active UI states.
- Use gold sparingly; avoid using it for large text blocks or every button.
- Use white and soft gray to create breathing room and make content easy to scan.
- Keep status colors distinct from brand colors so alerts are recognizable.
3. Status Colors
Status	Hex	Use
Success	#10B981	Successful actions and completed states
Warning	#F59E0B	Caution, pending review, or attention required
Error	#EF4444	Errors and destructive-action warnings
Information	#3B82F6	Informational notices and neutral guidance


Do not communicate status using color alone. Pair colors with labels or icons.
4. Typography
- Headings: Poppins
- Body text and UI: Inter
- Suggested weights: 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 600 Semibold, 700 Bold
Typography guidance
- Use bold, concise headings with a clear hierarchy.
- Keep paragraph line lengths comfortable for reading.
- Use consistent font sizes across equivalent components.
- Avoid using too many font weights or decorative typefaces.
5. UI Styling
Component	Styling guidance
Navbar	White background, navy text, emerald active indicator
Primary button	Emerald background with white text
Secondary button	White background with navy border and text
Cards	White surface, subtle border, restrained shadow
Links	Emerald, with a visible hover/focus state
Footer	Deep navy with white text and subtle emerald/gold details
Icons	Simple outline icons in navy or emerald
Card radius	12px
Button radius	8px
Shadows	Soft and minimal; avoid heavy glow effects
Implementation Notes
- Load Poppins and Inter in the project, with suitable system fallbacks.
- Define the palette as shared design tokens so the landing page and dashboard remain consistent.
- Check text/background contrast, especially for small text and buttons.
- Add visible keyboard focus states and accessible labels.
- Use real, approved project information and imagery; do not imply official endorsement or verified outcomes without evidence.
- Treat this document as the baseline brand guide. Component-level details may evolve while keeping the core palette consistent.
Brand summary: Deep navy communicates trust, emerald represents participation and growth, and civic gold highlights leadership and achievement. White and soft gray keep the experience clean, readable, and modern.
